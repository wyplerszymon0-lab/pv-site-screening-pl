*Average year*: the screening's energy, from the 2001–2020 climatology. *P50*: the mean of the yearly totals 2001–2025. *P90*: the energy exceeded in 9 years out of 10, P50 − 1.2816 σ (normal approximation); the empirical 10th percentile is the check on that assumption.

**Correlation.** Summing the gminas' P90s gives 43,638 GWh; the P90 of the powiat's yearly total is 43,649 GWh, almost the same. Pooling only narrows the spread when sites have different poor years; independent sites would pool to a P90 well above the sum of their P90s. Here 2 distinct NASA POWER irradiance series cover the whole powiat (its grid is about 1°), so the gminas are close to perfectly correlated by construction. Real weather 40 km apart is strongly but not perfectly correlated, so the true pooled P90 is a little higher.

**What this P90 leaves out.** Only weather variability. A bankable P90 adds the uncertainty of the yield model and of the satellite irradiance, a few percent each, which would lower it.
